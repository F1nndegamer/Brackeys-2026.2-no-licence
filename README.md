Monkey Daycare
Trust no monkey

Welcome to Monkey Daycare! Your job is really simple. Keep the monkeys fed, happy, and clean.

Beware, they are all a little naughty!


THE GAME
You are looking after 6 monkeys in your jungle. 

Jump between security camera feeds and keep an eye on what's happening in each zone. Your tasks include: 

Feeding hungry monkeys to raise their trust
Breaking up fights
Cleaning up after them
Keeping naughty monkeys under control
Sending troublemakers to time-out
Every now and then, a very naughty monkey will commit a crime.

You have just 5 seconds to quickly scan the camera feeds to gather information before being taken to a criminal lineup to select who you think is the guilty monkey!

WHO DID IT?



Four suspects will be put in front of you.

One of them is guilty.

If you saw the crime happen, great! Pick the culprit and carry on.

If you didn't see the crime, the monkeys will point fingers at each other.

Every monkey has a Trustworthiness meter. A monkey with 90% trustworthiness will tell the truth most of the time. A monkey with 10% trustworthiness is probably pointing at whoever they feel like.

And remember:

Being naughty is not being dishonest
A badly behaved monkey can still tell the truth
A perfectly calm monkey can still be a filthy liar
Choose correctly and the culprit goes to the Monkey Zoolag for life
Send an innocent monkey to the Zoolag? GAME OVER.

NAUGHTINESS
Naughtiness constantly grows. The more monkeys you have, the faster things get out of hand.

Naughty monkeys:

Move faster
Fight more often
Are more likely to commit crimes
Are more likely to cause a big mess!
But being naughty doesn't make a monkey a liar.

You can reduce naughtiness by putting a monkey into time-out shortly after they misbehave. Time-out a monkey when he hasn't been naughty, and he will come back with a vengeance.

TIME-OUT

Caught a monkey doing something naughty?

Select them and press the time-out button.
They'll spend 30 seconds in the time-out room.
If they recently committed a naughty action, you will reduce their naughtiness.
But be careful.

You can only have limited monkeys in time-out at one time.

And sending a perfectly innocent monkey to time-out will make them more naughty.
